# Personas and Journeys

> Selo 🟡 PLANEJADO em todos os itens.

## Persona 1: Driver
- **Profile:** 🟡 Car owner with an LED strip, door-handle and cup-holder RGB LEDs, and optionally an iPixel panel inside the car.
- **Context:** 🟡 Phone mounted on the dashboard holder with LED Union open, while driving, parked at night or at events.
- **Technical level:** 🟡 Advanced, in LED hardware, BLE devices and apps.
- **Main pain:** 🟡 Each light needs its own vendor app; no single place to control strip, RGB LEDs and panel together, and nothing reacts to the drive.
- **End goal:** 🟡 Practicality and a custom visual: one tap sets everything, and lights/panel react to movement.

### Main journey
1. 🟡 Open LED Union on the phone in the holder.
2. 🟡 Connect strip, RGB LEDs and iPixel with "Connect next".
3. 🟡 Apply a driving scene with one tap.
4. 🟡 Enable motion mode: lights and iPixel effects react to GPS speed, accelerometer and G-force.
5. 🟡 Tap a custom action button to fire a GIF or scene on the panel (e.g. a reaction GIF).
6. 🟡 Turn on stealth mode with one tap to switch everything off (traffic stop, parking).
7. 🟡 Turn stealth off and return to the last scene.

## Persona 2: Resident
- **Profile:** 🟡 Home owner with an iPixel LED matrix on the balcony showing the house number and fun effects (e.g. cat eyes appearing).
- **Context:** 🟡 At home, occasionally changing what the balcony panel shows.
- **Technical level:** 🟡 Advanced, in LED devices and apps.
- **Main pain:** 🟡 The vendor app UI is poor and inflexible for changing content dynamically and adding new effects.
- **End goal:** 🟡 A balcony panel with home identity and humour, changed dynamically through a much better interface.

### Main journey
1. 🟡 Open LED Union at home.
2. 🟡 Connect the balcony iPixel.
3. 🟡 Pick saved content (house number, cat eyes).
4. 🟡 Create or edit an effect (text, drawing, GIF, animation) with preview.
5. 🟡 Build a playlist of GIFs and texts with order, per-item duration and loop, more flexible than the vendor app.
6. 🟡 Send the playlist to the matrix and let it play on its own.
7. 🟡 Save items and playlist to the library.

---

## Persona 3: Content creator
- **Profile:** 🟡 Person who makes panel content for humour and identity, sourcing GIFs from the internet and live data from APIs.
- **Context:** 🟡 On the phone, browsing GIFs and checking social channels (own channel, X, live streams).
- **Technical level:** 🟡 Advanced, in image editing / pixel content and consuming APIs.
- **Main pain:** 🟡 The vendor app cannot show live data (followers, posts, live comments) nor easily import internet GIFs.
- **End goal:** 🟡 Humour and identity on the panel, including live social data that updates on its own.

### Main journey
1. 🟡 Find a GIF on the internet from the phone.
2. 🟡 Import the GIF into LED Union and fit it to the matrix (crop, scale, preview).
3. 🟡 Create a text with style and scroll effect.
4. 🟡 Bind an item to a data source, e.g. "Followers: {n}" from an API (channel, X followers/posts, live comments).
5. 🟡 Set the refresh interval.
6. 🟡 Add the item to a playlist and send to the matrix.
7. 🟡 See the panel update on its own when the data changes.
